Started with a kettle and a drawer of old letters

Meher opened the first counter in 2019, combining her grandmother's chai recipes with a lifelong habit of writing to people she missed. What began as four stools and one kettle is now a small room where students, writers, and slow mornings all find a seat.

We still brew in small batches, still keep a box of postcards by the door, and still close the register for anyone who wants to finish a paragraph before paying.

Before you visit

A few things people usually ask at the counter.

Do you have Wi-Fi and plug points?

Yes — Wi-Fi is free, and every table along the window has a plug point. We only ask that video calls happen outside.

Can I just come to write, without ordering chai?

Of course. The letter-writing table stays open through the day, no order required — though most people end up ordering anyway.

How do I book a Sunday workshop seat?

Send us a message using the contact form below with the workshop name and date, and we'll hold a seat for you.

Are dogs allowed inside?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the outdoor bench seats. There's usually a bowl of water waiting by the door.
