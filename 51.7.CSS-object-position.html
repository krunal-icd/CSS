CSS Object-Position Property

14 Practical Interactive Examples

1. Center Position

Balanced cropping

object-position: center
Image centered in container. Crops equally from all sides when used with object-fit: cover.
object-position: center;
50% 50%
✓ Most common position

2. Top Position

Crops from bottom

object-position: top
Focus on top of image. Perfect for face-focused photos where heads are at the top.
object-position: top;
50% 0%
✓ Face-focused photos

3. Bottom Position

Crops from top

object-position: bottom
Focus on bottom of image. Ideal for landscape photos with horizon or important foreground.
object-position: bottom;
50% 100%
✓ Landscape/horizon photos

4. Left Position

Crops from right

object-position: left
Focus on left side of image. Use for subjects positioned on the left with empty space.
object-position: left;
0% 50%
✓ Left-aligned subjects

5. Right Position

Crops from left

object-position: right
Focus on right side of image. Use for subjects positioned on the right with empty space.
object-position: right;
100% 50%
✓ Right-aligned subjects

6. Top Center

Top focus, centered

object-position: top center
Combines top focus with horizontal centering. Popular for portrait/face photos in hero sections.
object-position: top center;
50% 0%
✓ Portrait hero sections

7. Center Bottom

Bottom focus, centered

object-position: center bottom
Bottom focus with horizontal centering. Perfect for landscape with prominent horizon or sky above.
object-position: center bottom;
50% 100%
✓ Landscape with sky

8. Custom Percentage

Precise positioning

object-position: 75% 25%
Use percentages for exact positioning. 75% right, 25% down. Maximum control for off-center subjects.
object-position: 75% 25%;
Right-Top Focus
✓ Custom subject placement

9. Circular Profile Picture

Avatar crop

object-fit: cover + border-radius
Perfect circular crop using object-fit: cover and border-radius: 50%. Object-position ensures face is centered.
border-radius: 50%; object-fit: cover; object-position: center;
✓ Avatar/profile images

10. Hero Banner

Full-width hero

object-fit: cover + top focus
Hero image spanning full width. Object-position: center top keeps faces visible while covering full width.
width: 100%; height: 400px; object-fit: cover; object-position: center top;
✓ Hero/banner sections

11. Contain + Position

Full image centered

object-fit: contain
Image fits completely inside container. Object-position centers it with white space around. Great for products.
object-fit: contain; object-position: center;
✓ Product galleries

12. Thumbnail Grid Item

Video thumbnail crop

object-position: 50% 0%
Thumbnail focuses on top portion (video usually starts at top). Perfect for YouTube-style grids.
object-position: 50% 0%;
Top Focus
✓ Video thumbnails

13. Left Center

Subject on left

object-position: left center
Left-aligned with vertical centering. Good for compositions with empty space on right side.
object-position: left center;
0% 50%
✓ Left-aligned layouts

14. Right Center

Subject on right

object-position: right center
Right-aligned with vertical centering. Good for compositions with empty space on left side.
object-position: right center;
100% 50%
✓ Right-aligned layouts

Quick Reference Guide

Keywords: center | top | bottom | left | right | top center | center bottom | etc.
Percentages: 0% to 100% (e.g., 50% 50% = center)
Pixels: 10px 20px (fixed offsets)
Mixed: right 10px bottom 20px (keywords + measurements)

✓ Combine with object-fit for maximum control
✓ Use center as default for balanced cropping
✓ Use top/bottom for directional focus
✓ Use percentages for precise positioning
✓ Test with different aspect ratios
