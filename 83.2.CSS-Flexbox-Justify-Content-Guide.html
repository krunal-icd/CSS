💡 Key Takeaways

space-between: Use when you want elements pushed to opposite ends (navbars, headers, footers)
center: Use when you want to center content (hero sections, modals, showcase items)
space-around: Use for feature cards or product listings where you want even spacing
space-evenly: Use for statistics or metrics where perfect spacing matters
flex-start: Use when items should naturally align to the left (breadcrumbs, lists)
flex-end: Use for buttons or actions that should be on the right (save buttons, form controls)
🎨 Responsive Tip: Always combine with flex-wrap: wrap and gap for mobile-friendly designs. Test your layouts on different screen sizes!
